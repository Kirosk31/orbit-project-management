# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Orbit contributors are expected to make collaboration safe, respectful, and constructive. Be considerate, give actionable technical feedback, assume good intent while addressing concrete harm, and welcome people regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality, religion, or orientation.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, deliberate humiliation, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community spaces, and public interactions made while representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant according to the severity and pattern of behavior.

Maintainers should protect reporter confidentiality as far as practical, evaluate evidence consistently, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate enforcement decisions clearly to affected parties.

## Good review behavior

- Critique the code and decision, not the person.
- Explain security, accessibility, compatibility, or maintenance impact with evidence.
- Distinguish required changes from optional preferences.
- Do not pressure anyone to disclose private credentials, data, or personal circumstances.
- Pause a discussion that is becoming hostile and involve a maintainer.
